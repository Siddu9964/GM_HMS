3. IPD Module & Automated Billing Engine

Details how the system automatically tracks and accumulates charges for Ramesh Kumar (IPD000123) every day he stays in the hospital, ensuring zero revenue leakage.

                sequenceDiagram
                    autonumber
                    actor R as Receptionist
                    participant DB as Central Database
                    participant BE as Auto Billing Engine
                    actor N as Nurse / Depts
                    actor A as Admin

                    R->>DB: Admit Ramesh (Bed 101, IPD000123)
                    DB-->>R: Admission Created (Bed Status: Occupied)
                    R->>BE: Generate Initial Bill Master
                    BE-->>DB: Creates BILL-2026-001 (Status: OPEN)
                    
                    Note over BE, DB: Daily Automated Background Cycle
                    loop Every 24 Hours
                        BE->>DB: Check Days since Admission
                        BE->>DB: Insert ROOM_RENT charge (Bed Charge + Food)
                    end
                    
                    N->>BE: Add Lab/Pharmacy/Doctor charges for Ramesh
                    BE->>DB: Insert Itemized Billing Charge
                    BE->>BE: Trigger recalculateMaster()
                    BE-->>DB: Update Bill Subtotal & Apply Taxes
                    
                    A->>BE: Review Final Bill for Discharge
                    BE->>DB: Apply Corporate Discount / Insurance
                    A->>BE: Record Final Payment Received
                    BE->>DB: Update Bill (Status: FINALIZED)

4. Laboratory (LIS) Module Decision Flow

How a blood test requested for Ramesh moves through the laboratory module, including the critical alert decision tree.

                flowchart TD
                    Order["🩺 Doctor/Nurse:\nOrders CBC Test"]:::start --> Pending["⏳ Lab Dashboard:\nTest Appears in Pending"]:::lab
                    Pending --> Collect["🩸 Lab Tech:\nCollects Blood Sample"]:::lab
                    Collect --> InProgress["🔬 Status:\nChanged to In-Progress"]:::lab
                    InProgress --> Analyze["⚙️ Lab Tech:\nAnalyzes in Machine"]:::lab
                    Analyze --> DataEntry["⌨️ Lab Tech:\nEnter Results (Hb, WBC)"]:::lab
                    
                    DataEntry --> Validation{"✅ Values in\nNormal Range?"}
                    Validation -->|"Yes"| Complete["🏁 Status:\nCompleted"]:::lab
                    Validation -->|"No"| CheckCritical{"⚠️ Is Value\nCritical? (e.g. Hb < 7)"}
                    
                    CheckCritical -->|"No, just abnormal"| Complete
                    CheckCritical -->|"Yes, Critical"| Alert["🚨 System generates\nCritical Alert (Red)"]:::alert
                    Alert --> Notify["📞 Lab Tech:\nCalls Doctor immediately"]:::alert
                    Notify --> Complete
                    
                    Complete --> Report["📄 System:\nAuto-generates PDF Report"]:::finish

5. Pharmacy Module FIFO Inventory Flow

Shows how the pharmacy manages stock levels automatically and ensures the oldest medicines are given to Ramesh first to prevent expiry wastage.

                flowchart TD
                    subgraph Receiving Stock
                        Vendor["Supplier Delivers Paracetamol"] --> GRN["Pharmacist Creates Goods Receipt Note"]
                        GRN --> BatchA["Batch A: Expires Dec 2026"]
                        GRN --> BatchB["Batch B: Expires Jun 2027"]
                        BatchA --> Inv[("Pharmacy Central Inventory")]
                        BatchB --> Inv
                    end
                    
                    subgraph Dispensing to Ramesh
                        Order["Nurse orders 15 tablets for Ramesh"] --> CheckStock["System checks Inventory Levels"]
                        CheckStock --> FIFO{"FIFO Logic Applied Automatically"}
                        FIFO -->|Deducts first from Oldest| TakeA["Takes 10 tabs from Batch A"]
                        FIFO -->|Deducts remaining from Next| TakeB["Takes 5 tabs from Batch B"]
                        TakeA & TakeB --> Bill["Cost automatically added to Ramesh's IPD Bill"]
                        Bill --> Dispense["Medicines dispatched to Ward"]
                    end
                    
                    Inv --> CheckStock
